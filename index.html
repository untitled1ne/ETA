E.T.A. – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026
Developer: untitledone
Contact: hi.untitledone@gmail.com

This policy explains what information the game E.T.A. (package com.eta.deliverygame) handles. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, coins, Career earnings, unlocked jobs, settings, chosen city and language) is saved only on your phone. If Android backup is turned on, Android may back this progress up to your own Google account so it can be restored when you reinstall. We never receive it.

Maps

To draw the city you play in, the game downloads map data from OpenStreetMap-based services (OpenFreeMap tiles, Overpass and Photon search). These requests contain the area of the map being shown or the place name you search for, and, like any internet request, your IP address. The game does not use your phone's GPS location.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads, including optional rewarded ads, through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about ad interactions and app performance, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. In regions where it is required (for example the EEA and UK), you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Privacy › Ads).

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminder notifications on your phone. They are created on the device; no data is sent to us.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. We do not collect personal information ourselves; data processed by Google AdMob is handled under Google's privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game removes all progress stored on your phone. To remove a backup, delete the app's backup in your Google account (Google Drive › Backups) or turn off backup for the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
